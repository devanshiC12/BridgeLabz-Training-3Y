Overview

Manali is a hill town in Himachal Pradesh, known for snow-capped mountains, pine forests, and the Beas river flowing through the valley.

Things To Do

Visit Solang Valley for paragliding, explore Hidimba Temple, or take a day trip to Rohtang Pass for the snow.

Local Food

Try Siddu, Trout fish curry, and hot Himachali tea after a day of sightseeing in the cold mountain air.

Travel Tips

Carry warm clothing even in summer, book stays in advance during peak season, and check road conditions before heading to Rohtang.
